Our learning approach

Discovery before pressure

Preschool learning works best when children feel safe, curious, and capable. MindAgo is designed around warm invitations and meaningful practice—not speed, streaks, or high-pressure rewards.

“A library where children learn through exploration and play.”
  • 01

    Wonder before instruction

    Begin with curiosity, character, or discovery.

  • 02

    Confidence before difficulty

    Help children feel capable before increasing challenge.

  • 03

    Visual learning before symbols

    Build meaning with objects and groups before abstract notation.

  • 04

    Real-world follow-up activities

    Every content activity can inspire a simple off-screen mission.

  • 05

    Learning should feel like discovery

    The experience invites exploration instead of presenting a subject menu.

For parents

Clear choices for a calmer first experience

The current MVP is intentionally small. Families can understand what it does, what it saves, and what is not part of the product.

  • Short, preschool-friendly sessions
  • Clear educational objectives
  • Progress saved locally on the device
  • No advertising
  • No shop or child-facing purchases
  • No AI mentor in the current MVP
  • No child photo uploads
✓

Built for confidence

MindAgo uses encouraging language, retry-friendly activities, and age-appropriate visual design. Progress is there to support the learner—not to rank or pressure them.

Parents or guardians should remain nearby during private alpha sessions.
