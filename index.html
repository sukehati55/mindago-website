Mathematics · Ages 4–6

Comparing Quantities & Counting

Children count apples into baskets and compare groups to understand more, fewer, and equal quantities. Professor Mindy speaks instructions aloud with gentle retry cues.

  • Spoken instructions with instant replay
  • Concrete tactile quantities before abstract numerals
  • Encouraging star rewards without timers
English · Phonics

Phonics & Sound Blending

Early readers listen to phonemes and slide letter tiles into place to build words. Clear pronunciation and visual rewards turn early reading practice into a game.

  • Clear human pronunciation of each letter sound
  • Interactive drag-and-drop letter slots
  • Vocabulary reinforced with delightful illustrations
Creative Easel · Motor Skills

Shape Tracing & Colouring

Smooth finger or stylus tracing helps young children gain fine motor control. Activities include tracing geometric shapes, completing whimsical pictures, and creative colour mixing.

  • Forgiving paths that encourage natural motor flow
  • 6 progressive drawing and colouring modes
  • Calm sensory feedback without sudden loud buzzers
Building Blocks · Spatial Logic

Tactile Spatial Puzzles

Children drag quadrant tiles and multi-piece shapes to assemble cheerful animals and familiar everyday objects, discovering how parts combine to create a whole.

  • 6 unique spatial puzzle variations
  • Snaps smoothly into place when correctly matched
  • Celebrates every success with Migo’s joyful dance
Bookshelf · Early Literacy

Read-and-Listen Picture Books

Eight interactive illustrated books let children flip pages at their own pace, tap any picture or word to hear clear spoken audio, and explore end-of-book Look Again! glossaries.

  • 8 full picture books across animals, fruits, and stories
  • Tap-to-hear word pronunciation and semantic cues
  • Zero background advertisements or purchase interruptions
Progress & Motivation

Calm Growth & Parent Records

As your child completes activities, the Knowledge Egg in the Playroom nest gently grows and cracks to reveal companion surprises. Scored summaries sync to the early Parent Dashboard.

  • Local on-device saving keeps progress safe without internet
  • Early Parent Dashboard tracks scored lesson milestones
  • No public leaderboards, competitive ranks, or countdown timers

Our learning approach

Discovery before pressure

Preschool learning works best when children feel safe, curious, and capable. MindAgo uses spoken guidance, tactile drag-and-drop play, and creative expression—not timers or high-pressure rewards.

“A playroom where children build confidence through exploration, stories, and play.”
  • 01

    Wonder before instruction

    Begin with curiosity, character, and inviting Playroom objects.

  • 02

    Confidence before difficulty

    Forgiving retries and spoken replay help children succeed at their own pace.

  • 03

    Visual and hands-on meaning

    Count objects, blend sounds, trace paths, and build shapes before abstract drills.

  • 04

    Real-world follow-up ideas

    Parent records highlight simple off-screen activities and printable practice.

  • 05

    Learning should feel like discovery

    Guided lessons and free exploration sit side by side in the same room.
