Made for tracing practice

Minda Dots is a dotted tracing font, rather than a set of ready-made worksheets. Type a supported letter, number or name, then arrange it in your own page. You choose the size, repetition and writing space to suit the activity.

The dots provide a visible path for guided practice. An adult can still demonstrate the movement and talk through where to begin; a font alone does not teach handwriting or indicate stroke order.

Alphabet sheets: pair a capital and lowercase letter with room to trace.
Number practice: prepare short rows of the numbers 0–9.
Name tracing: personalise a page with a supported name.
Early handwriting: focus on a few familiar letters at a time.
Classroom printables: repeat an example in your own worksheet layout.
Homeschool pages: adjust the size and amount of practice for your learner.

Character coverage

Both fonts include uppercase A–Z, lowercase a–z, numbers 0–9 and space. Minda Teaching Plain additionally includes !. Accented letters and other punctuation are not part of this verified character set; check a name before planning a worksheet.

Pair it with a clear teaching model

  1. Show the clean letter model with Minda Teaching Plain.
  2. Provide the same letters in Minda Dots.
  3. Let the learner trace and practise, with guidance as needed.

Keep the model and tracing example close together so the learner can refer back to the solid letter. Leave an empty line when you want to offer copying practice too.
