Designed for short, purposeful practice

  1. Choose one page or one movement pattern at a time.
  2. Show the pencil movement before the learner starts.
  3. Encourage slow, steady tracing rather than rushing to finish.
  4. Reuse favourite pages in a dry-erase sleeve or laminate selected sheets.
  5. Use the included certificate when the learner completes the adventure.

Colour printing is recommended because the worksheets use colourful cues and playful illustrations, although the tracing paths remain the main focus.
