Show the example before the practice

A solid model gives you a clear reference to point to while discussing a letter. Place one large example at the top of a practice page, or display a short word during a demonstration. Choose a size that leaves each letter easy to see.

Use the model alongside your own explanation of letter formation. The font supplies the letter shape; your demonstration supplies the movement, starting point and pace.

Teacher demonstrations: display a large letter or familiar word.
Worksheet instructions: add a short, supported phrase such as Copy me!
Classroom examples: label a practice area with a solid letter model.
Letter formation pages: keep a clean reference beside the writing space.
Homeschool activities: type familiar names or simple words.
Early-writing resources: combine a model with space for independent attempts.

Character coverage

Both fonts include uppercase A–Z, lowercase a–z, numbers 0–9 and space. Minda Teaching Plain additionally includes !. Accented letters and other punctuation are not part of this verified character set; check a name before planning a worksheet.
