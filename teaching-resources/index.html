Frequently asked questions

Can I sell worksheets made with the font?

Yes. The Standard Commercial Desktop License allows finished or flattened commercial resources. The font file itself must not be redistributed.

Can I use the fonts in Canva, Word or PowerPoint?

Yes when your software or account supports installing or uploading custom fonts.

Can I share the font file with a client or colleague?

No. Anyone who needs to install or directly use the font needs their own appropriate license.

Can I use the font inside an app or website?

Not under the included desktop license. App, software, webfont and other embedding uses require a separate license.

Is this a physical product?

No. Minda Teaching Fonts are digital downloads delivered through Payhip.

Who are these resources for?

Teachers, parents, homeschoolers and educational resource creators working with early learners.
