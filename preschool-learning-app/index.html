MindAgo Playroom · Ages 4–6

An Educational Learning App for Preschoolers

MindAgo Playroom brings early maths, English phonics, tracing, stories and spatial puzzles together in one interactive, play-based preschool learning app.

Children explore familiar learning stations with spoken guidance from Professor Mindy and Migo. The activities focus on early academic skills through approachable interactions—not just entertainment.

What children learn

Preschool skills, presented through play

Each learning station combines a playful interaction with recognizable early-learning goals.

Early Maths

Count, match and compare quantities through visual, interactive activities at the Math Train.

English & Phonics

Explore letter sounds, sound blending and spoken early-language prompts with gentle retries.

Tracing & Creativity

Practise lines, shapes, tracing and creative expression in Trace & Draw and Colour & Paint.

Spatial Thinking

Explore shapes, positions and early spatial concepts through tactile Building Blocks puzzles.

Stories & Listening

Discover read-and-listen picture books in the Bookshelf and language activities in the Story Corner.

Calm, Guided Practice

Professor Mindy and Migo help children try again without countdown pressure or leaderboards.

Designed for young learners

Why an educational app should feel like discovery

Preschoolers are building the foundations of listening, counting, early literacy and spatial thinking. MindAgo uses familiar objects, simple spoken instructions and opportunities to retry. Parents can follow up with hands-on counting, shape hunts, drawing or printable practice.

Our goal is to complement—never replace—parent involvement, teaching or healthy off-screen activities.

Questions from parents

Preschool app questions, answered

What age is MindAgo Playroom for?

The current Playroom is designed for preschool children aged 4, 5 and 6. Parents can choose activities suited to their child's pace.

Is MindAgo Playroom an educational app or just a game?

It is an educational learning app. The playful environment leads to activities in Mathematics, English, tracing, creativity and spatial reasoning.

Does a child need to read to use the app?

Many activities include spoken guidance for children who are still learning to read. Parents may help with setup and the first sessions.

How does Founding 50 early access work?

The Founding 50 offer is US$4.99 once for six calendar months, with no automatic renewal and a limit of 49 paying families. Parents must confirm their email and record parental privacy consent before purchasing with that same email. Payment and eligibility are processed automatically; check the parent portal for Play Now, and contact support if access is delayed. Do not pay a second time.
